Egg Recipes & Nutrition - Longer

Exotic Eggs 12k Tray: Good for chapati business - beat 2 eggs, add salt, chopped onions tomatoes, pour on chapati dough fry 2 mins. For cake - 4 eggs + flour + sugar + margarine, bake 45 mins makes 1kg cake sell 30k profit 15k. 1 tray = 30 eggs makes 15 chapatis profit 10,500. Storage: keep in tray in cool place not fridge, use within 2 weeks.

Local Eggs 22k Tray: More nutritious Omega-3, deep yellow yolk, no chemicals, good for kids brain development, pregnant mothers. Boil 10 mins, or fry with tomatoes onions 3 mins. Why 22k vs 12k? Local hen lays 1 egg per 2 days vs exotic daily, natural feed expensive.

Freshness Check: Egg in water - sinks flat very fresh 1-2 days, sinks stands up fresh 1 week, floats old not fresh. Our eggs fresh collected daily from Kitoro HQ farm.

How to Rear Chicks - Longer Guide

Housing: Need house 3x3ft for 50 chicks, dry, no wind, clean daily, litter wood shavings 2 inches thick. Brooder for exotic day-old: bulb 100W heat 32°C first week, reduce 2°C per week. Local 1 month no need heat already hardened.

Feeds: Exotic starter 0-2 weeks 1kg per chick protein 20%, grower 2-5 weeks 2kg protein 18%, finisher 5 weeks+ 2kg protein 16%. Local starter 0-4 weeks 1kg, grower 4-8 weeks 2kg, then free-range + maize 50g daily + greens + mukene. Water clean always, vitamins first 5 days, glucose first day.

Vaccines: We already vaccinated Marek day 1, Newcastle day 7 eye drop, Gumboro day 14 drinking water. Next Newcastle day 21, 28. We give guide book free.
